# Mailon

Mailon is a mail app for the Mac. It puts all your accounts in one quick inbox, helps you write with Kalon running on your own Mac, and keeps your mail encrypted on your device.

[Download Mailon 1.0](https://github.com/KalonEdge/Mailon/releases/latest/download/Mailon-1.0.dmg) for macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

## Features

- All your IMAP accounts together, or one at a time. iCloud, Gmail, Yahoo, Fastmail and most company mail servers work; iCloud and Gmail need an app password from your account settings.
- Snooze, send later and undo send.
- Conversations, rules, labels, templates and signatures.
- One-click unsubscribe from mailing lists.
- Help with writing: turn a few notes into a full message or reply, make a draft shorter or more formal, or summarise a long thread. With Kalon it all happens on your Mac. You can also connect another provider of your choice.
- Remote images and tracking pixels are blocked until you load them.
- Keyboard shortcuts from both Gmail and Apple Mail, plus light and dark mode and VoiceOver.

## Install

Download the disk image from the [latest release](https://github.com/KalonEdge/Mailon/releases/latest), open it, and drag Mailon into Applications. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

## Updates

Mailon looks for a new version once a day and asks before installing it. Each update is signed, and Mailon won't install one that isn't. You can also choose Check for Updates from the Mailon menu.

## Privacy

Your mail, drafts and settings are stored encrypted on your Mac, protected by the Secure Enclave on Macs that have one. Passwords are kept in your Keychain. Mailon connects only to your mail servers and, when you ask for writing help, to the service you picked. It has no analytics and no servers of its own.
